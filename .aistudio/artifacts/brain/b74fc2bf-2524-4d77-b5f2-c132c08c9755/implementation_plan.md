### Visual Identity & Curatorial Styling

- **Palette**:
  - `Canvas`: Deep archival green/black `#071A10` and `#0A2416` with warm paper accents `#F7F5EE`.
  - `Dividers`: Hairline 1px borders (`border-[#F7F5EE]/10`).
  - `Accent`: Electric chartreuse (`#B7F34A`) and warm gold (`#E0B86C`) for active states, playback indicators, and verified badges.
- **Typography**:
  - `Headings`: High-contrast editorial serif (`Newsreader` / `Playfair Display`) and bold condensed uppercase.
  - `Body`: Readable geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) constrained to 68ch reading measure.
  - `Numbers & Metrics`: `font-mono tabular-nums` for precise alignment.
- **Zero-Pill Discipline**: Static metadata rendered as unboxed text separated by subtle typographic bullets (`·`), while interactive category switchers use clean segmented buttons.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Full-Screen Video Background with Video URL Switcher**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Embed a high-quality video loop initialized on mute with autoplay, while providing an unobtrusive "Switch Video Source" drawer allowing Mark or visitors to swap between pre-selected Web3 conference clips or paste their own custom MP4 URL.
  - *Why*: Delivers an immediate cinematic hook while giving the user instant control over the video asset.
- **Decision 2: Interactive Editorial Chapters**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Structured dossier tabs (Education, Certificates, Events, Posts Held) that allow visitors to inspect verified credentials without long wall-of-text fatigue.
- **Decision 3: Dedicated Visual Proofs Gallery with Lightbox**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Curated collection of high-resolution photographic and video documentation with category filters and full-screen view mode.
- **Decision 4: Seamless Testimonials & Social Links**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Quote cards with direct links to the endorser's verified social media profile (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Telegram).
